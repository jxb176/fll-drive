Personal file transfer setup

FLL Drive

FLL Drive is a personal rclone setup operated by GitHub account owner jxb176 to transfer FIRST LEGO League (FLL) meeting materials between Linux and the owner's Google Drive, making finished files available from a laptop.

Materials may be prepared using Codex. Google authorization is handled by rclone; this informational website does not provide a sign-in form or perform Drive transfers.

This is a personal utility, not an FLL team website or an official FIRST service.

Privacy policy

Terms of use

This setup is intended for the account owner's personal use. The owner is responsible for choosing files, permissions, and transfer commands. No public file-transfer service is offered.
